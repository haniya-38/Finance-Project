OBJECTIVE: 

Using 14 years of Pakistan Stock Exchange data (2010–2024) and Google Trends search 
behavior, this project analyzes how economic stress — measured by inflation-related 
searches (Dollar Rate, Petrol Price) — correlates with stock performance across two 
groups: defensive companies (Nestle, Abbott) that should hold up, and cyclical companies
 (Lucky Cement, HBL) that should fall. All findings are visualized through normalized 
 charts and correlation analysis, with a forward-looking trend estimate.
 "When Dollar Rate searches spike (inflation panic), 
 do defensive stocks hold up while cyclical stocks fall?"

defensive approach; nestle, abbot, national foods-------remains stable in downturn
cyclic approach;  lucky cement, HBL, gul ahmed--------- decline with downturn
